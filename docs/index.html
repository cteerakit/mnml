How it works

Three steps to a calmer inbox and feed.

  1. Step 1

    Add mnml to Chrome

    Install it free from the Chrome Web Store.

  2. Step 2

    Open mnml

    Click the mnml icon in your browser toolbar to see all the options.

  3. Step 3

    Pick what to hide

    Turn options on or off for Gmail and YouTube. Most changes take effect right away.

Gmail & YouTube

See everything you can turn off for Gmail and YouTube. You control it all from mnml.

Gmail

Cut down visual noise in your inbox—narrow the layout, tuck away menus, and keep only what you need on screen.

  • Narrow your inbox width

    Pick a comfortable width for your inbox and messages—small, medium, large, or full screen like Gmail normally shows.

  • Hide the Gmail logo

    Remove the Gmail logo at the top of the page.

  • Hide the left menu until you need it

    Inbox, labels, and other items stay out of the way until you hover over the menu or open it. The Compose button can stay visible, or move to a floating button.

  • Floating Compose button

    Put Compose in a small button at the bottom-right corner instead of the left sidebar.

  • Hide the toolbar above your emails

    Select, Refresh, and other controls above your message list stay hidden until you move your mouse over that area.

  • Hide the add-ons panel

    Remove the panel on the right used for Gmail add-ons.

  • Hide the search bar

    Remove the search box at the top.

  • Use a smaller search bar

    Show a compact search field in the center that expands when you click or hover on it.

  • Hide top-right icons

    Hide Support, Settings, Studio, Gemini, and Google apps. Your profile picture stays visible. Hover that corner to bring the other icons back.

  • Hide footer links

    Remove links and promos at the bottom of Gmail.

YouTube

Focus on the video—hide Shorts, comments, suggestions, and other clutter. Most changes kick in as soon as you flip a switch.

  • Hide the left menu until you need it

    Give videos more room on screen. Move your mouse to the left edge or the menu button to open navigation when you want it.

  • Hide the Create button

    Remove the upload button at the top.

  • Hide notifications

    Remove the bell icon at the top.

  • Hide voice search

    Remove the microphone button next to search.

  • Hide the YouTube logo

    Remove the YouTube or Premium logo at the top.

  • Hide category filters

    Remove the filter buttons under search on the home page.

  • Hide Shorts

    Remove Shorts rows and Shorts links in the menu.

  • Hide comments

    Remove the comments section while watching a video.

  • Hide suggested videos

    Remove the list of recommended videos on the right.

  • Hide end screens

    Remove overlays and cards that appear at the end of a video.

Your privacy matters

mnml only works on Gmail and YouTube. We never read your emails, chats, or what you watch. We do not sell your information or track you. Your settings are saved on your computer and can sync across your devices if you use Chrome's sync.
